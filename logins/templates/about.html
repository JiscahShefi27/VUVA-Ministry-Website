ABOUT VUVA MINISTRIES

Mr. Bannet Singh J. M. founded the YUVA (Youth Under Vision of Almighty) Ministries in 2010 with a vision to reach youngsters and spread the Gospel through outreach, children’s ministry, prison ministry, and youth gatherings.

In 2012, the ministry was officially registered as Village Under Vision of Almighty Education and Charitable Trust and expanded its mission to Chidambaram village through the support of Mr. Allen Ferguson and Pastor Cibi.

The ministry focused on church planting and community development, establishing churches and empowering local believers.

In 2022, the organization evolved into two entities: VUVA Ministries for Gospel outreach and VUVA Charitable Trust for social service and humanitarian work.

Our Mission

To serve communities by spreading faith, uplifting lives, supporting missionaries, and nurturing spiritual growth through ministry programs and outreach initiatives.

Our Vision

Isaiah 43:10
